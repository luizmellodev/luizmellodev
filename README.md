# Luiz Mello

iOS Engineer in Porto Alegre, Brazil. I build native apps with SwiftUI and UIKit, care about the small UX details, and share what I learn along the way (usually with a coffee nearby).

Right now I'm at **TELUS Digital / WillowTree**, building iOS apps for large consumer products used by tens of millions of people. Work across telecom, news, and insurance.

I got here through the Apple Developer Academy. After that, I helped build a startup from the ground up and kept shipping side projects on my own.

**[luizmello.dev](https://luizmello.dev)** is the hub for everything else: articles, apps, and side projects.

### Things I've built and actually use
- **[TabNews Reader](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tabnews-reader/id6755933359)** · unofficial iOS client for [TabNews](https://www.tabnews.com.br). Feed, library, widgets, Apple Watch, newsletter notifications, Dev Games. And it's Open source! PRs welcome.
- **[Noira Cam](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/noira-cam/id6774428917)** · film-inspired camera and photo editor. Film looks in-camera, depth blur, the whole color grading rabbit hole.
- **[Ritus Club](https://ritus.club)** · specialty coffee guide I built after getting way too into espresso and latte art. Seed to cup, brew methods, tools.
- **[SwiftShelf](https://swiftshelf.luizmello.dev/)** · community library of SwiftUI components. Still a work in progress (and it's Open Source too!)
- **Content** · dev tips on [@luizmello.dev](https://www.instagram.com/luizmello.dev/) (Portuguese). Not super active lately.

On the side I poke at Next.js/React and Python when a problem asks for it.
